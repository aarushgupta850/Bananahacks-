# Ghost Lines: project description

For the submission form. Replace each [X] with a real number from one of our own runs.

Ghost Lines teaches you to draw, then proves you improved. Pick a subject (or type your own), copy it on
your own for 45 seconds, and Ghost Lines measures your drawing part by part: a head [X]% too big, a missing
tail, hesitant lines. It then teaches those weak spots with faint "ghost" guide lines on your canvas. The
guides follow wherever you are actually drawing, and fade from full lines to dots to nothing as you get better.
A retest and a side-by-side report card show the measured change (in our own test run, [X] → [X]).
Every lesson is made by image AI: SDXL-Lightning draws the line art, the pipeline traces it into strokes,
OWLv2 finds and labels the parts, and the strokes are ordered the way an artist draws. A "How this lesson was
made" panel shows each step. The whole app runs in the browser, so anyone can try it at
https://aarushgupta850.github.io/Bananahacks-/.

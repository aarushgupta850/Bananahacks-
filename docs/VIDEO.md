## Before recording

- [ ] One full run-through to get the real test and retest scores.
- [ ] Live server and tunnel running, and "Live AI" showing on the picker (otherwise cut the live generation
  shot). A prompt takes about 15 s and a photo about 18 to 20 s.
- [ ] Browser at 100% zoom, a clean profile without bookmarks or extensions, and notifications off.
- [ ] Use a pen or tablet if you have one: the lines look better on video than with a mouse.
